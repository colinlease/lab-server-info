Overview

Lab Server Monitor tracks the availability and operational health of a privately operated server. It sends email alerts, recovery notices, and daily system update summaries.

Monitoring coverage

  • Availability: an independent cloud service detects missing server reports.
  • Services and jobs: checks identify application failures, worker interruptions, and stalled background activity.
  • Backup health: reports show backup status and freshness.
  • System maintenance: daily summaries identify available updates and required restarts.

Email notifications

Alerts identify the affected component and the observed condition. Recovery notices confirm when fresh monitoring evidence shows that the condition has cleared. Unresolved incidents may receive reminders.

Lab Server Monitor uses an authorized Gmail account to deliver notifications to a configured recipient. The privacy policy explains Google access and data handling.

Personal use

Colin Lease operates Lab Server Monitor for his own systems. It has no public registration or subscription service. The terms of service describe authorized use and monitoring limitations.
